# Alleytale

**A guide for every street, not one route.**

Alleytale is an audio guide for any street. Start a walk, put the phone away, and it tells you short
stories about what you are passing, paced to how you walk.

- **Ambient narration.** No routes to record and nothing to ask: the guide decides when there is something
  worth telling, and goes quiet when there isn't.
- **Paced to you.** It speeds up, holds back, or offers a break when you stop for a while.
- **Personal.** Pick the voice, the tone and what you're curious about. It remembers across walks.
- **Plans the walk.** Tell the planner what you want to see and it builds the route.
- **Works with the screen off,** on Android and iOS, in 18 languages.
